# Third-party notices

Check the current terms before use beyond research.

- **MediaPipe Face Landmarker** (Google), Apache-2.0: `web/public/models/face_landmarker.task` and the WASM runtime in `web/public/mediapipe/`.
- **Head anatomy** (`web/public/models/head_anatomy.glb`): cut from the Ecorche model, **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Attribution and the changes made are in
  `web/public/models/NOTICE-head_anatomy.txt`. Derivatives of this file must keep the same licence.
- **Sample portraits** (`web/public/samples/`, `fixtures/faces/`): public-domain official portraits from Wikimedia Commons. Authors and sources are in each `ATTRIBUTION.json`.
  The example lesions drawn on them are synthetic; no patient data is included.
- **MobileSAM** (Zhang et al.), Apache-2.0, ONNX conversion by Acly (MIT): optional, downloaded by the user, never committed.
- **Three.js**, **postprocessing**, **GSAP**, **Lenis**, **MediaPipe tasks-vision**: used through npm under their own licences.
- The Python research package that the OSF and Zenodo records describe is in the tag `python-package-v0.1.0`; its notices are in that tag.
