What this cannot do

  • Scale without a reference object is an estimate, usually within a third of the true size.
  • Depth cannot be measured from a photograph. Any depth in cm is a range, not a reading.
  • A photograph cannot exclude malignancy. Suspicious lesions need a biopsy first.
  • No beauty score and no verdict on whether surgery is needed.
FaceStudio is decision support for clinicians. It does not diagnose. Landmarks by MediaPipe Face Landmarker. Everything else is computed in your browser.
